Privacy Policy

Topple, a game by Topple Studio · Last updated 17 October 2026

This policy explains what data the game Topple (Android and iOS) handles, why, and the choices you have. Topple has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or any other personal details.

Who we are

Topple is made by Topple Studio. Questions or requests about your data: topplestd@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your game progress is saved only on your phone or tablet and is never sent to us:

  • level results (stars and best heights), your endless-mode best and your place on the map;
  • your boosters, the story postcards you have read and your sound setting;
  • whether you own No Ads.

If your device backs up app data (Android backup or iCloud), this save may be included in that backup under your device settings.

Advertising: Google AdMob

Topple is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: an ad between some levels, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (to continue a run or get a booster). To show and measure ads, AdMob collects:

  • the advertising identifier of your device (on iOS only if you allow tracking, see below);
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location is derived;
  • ad interactions and performance diagnostics.

Google uses this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase

To learn which levels are too hard or too easy, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase: a level or run starting and ending (level, height reached, stars, time played), boosters used, ads shown, purchases started, completed or failed, and settings changed. Firebase also records an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system and country (derived from your IP address). These events are not linked to your name or any account. Event-level data is deleted automatically after 2 months.

Purchases

The optional No Ads purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Your payment details never reach us; the game only receives confirmation that the purchase was made, so it can remove ads. Restore Purchases in Settings asks the store for that confirmation again.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). Before any personalised ads or analytics, Google’s consent form asks for your choice. Analytics stay off until you answer. You can change your choice at any time: tap the gear on the map, then Privacy Choices.
  • Tracking on iOS. The game asks once whether it may track you across apps (App Tracking Transparency). If you don’t allow it, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised with it. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Advertising ID on Android. You can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Google (or Privacy) → Ads.
  • Deleting your data. Uninstalling Topple deletes the save on your device and stops all collection by the game. As there are no accounts, we can’t link analytics or ad data to you, but if you contact us we will help with any request to access or delete data, as far as we are able.

Children

Topple is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the game and want data removed, contact us and we will help.

Security

The services above send their data over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We don’t run our own servers and receive no personal data from the game.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
